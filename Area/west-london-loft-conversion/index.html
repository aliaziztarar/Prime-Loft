Local Planning

Loft Conversion Planning Rules in West London

Understanding the planning rules specific to your area in West London is essential before starting a loft conversion project.

Permitted Development

Most houses in West London retain Permitted Development rights, allowing a rear dormer of up to 40m³ (terraced) or 50m³ (detached/semi) without a full planning application.

Conservation Areas

Many streets in West London are within designated conservation areas where any visible roof alteration requires a full planning application.

Height Limits

No part of a PD conversion can exceed the height of the existing ridge. Our surveyors calculate your specific allowance at the free visit.

Building Regulations

All loft conversions in West London require Building Regulations approval regardless of planning status. We manage this as part of your fixed price.
