Our Process

How We Deliver Your Dream Loft

A transparent, fully managed process from your very first enquiry right through to final handover — you're always in the picture, always in control.

01

Free Survey

An experienced surveyor visits your home — evenings and weekends available. We assess your roof, measure the space, check planning constraints, and identify the best conversion option for your property.

02

Fixed-Price Quote

Within days you receive a fully itemised fixed-price quotation. No ballpark figures — the exact price you'll pay, including architecture, structural engineering, all planning applications, full build, and 10-year warranty.

03

Design & Approvals

Our architects draw up your plans. We manage all planning and Building Regulations applications on your behalf. Your dedicated project manager is assigned and coordinates every stage.

04

The Build

Our specialist team starts on site. Most loft conversions take 6–10 weeks. Daily site visits from your project manager. You're always kept in the picture.

05

Handover

Full snagging inspection with you before we leave. Building Regulations Completion Certificate and 10-year structural warranty issued on handover day.

The Prime Difference

The price we quote is the price you pay — written into your contract. No variation orders mid-build, no extras at handover. This commitment is absolute, not aspirational.

Every Prime Loft client has a single named project manager from survey to handover. On site daily. Available by phone throughout. You are never left wondering what is happening.

We do not outsource design or structural engineering. Our in-house architects and engineers produce every drawing and calculation — ensuring design quality and structural integrity from the outset.

Every Prime Loft conversion comes with a fully backed 10-year structural warranty — the same protection as a new build home. Your investment is protected long after we leave site.
